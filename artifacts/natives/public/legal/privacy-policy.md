# Privacy Policy

**Impact Natives Ltd**
Last updated: June 2025

---

## 1. Who We Are

Impact Natives Ltd ("Impact Natives", "we", "us", or "our") is a company registered in Nigeria. We operate the Impact Natives platform at [impactnatives.com](https://impactnatives.com) and [app.impactnatives.com](https://app.impactnatives.com), a social impact coordination and partnership platform connecting NGOs, funders, corporates, creatives, and ecosystem builders across Africa.

For questions about this policy, contact us at: **contact@impactnatives.com**

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

### Information you provide directly
- Full name, email address, phone number
- Organisation name, role title, organisation type
- Country and location
- Bio, profile photo, website, LinkedIn URL, and other social links
- Sectors of interest and focus areas
- Expressions of interest, messages, and conversation content
- Verification documents (for organisation accounts)
- Lab commission requests and associated content
- Newsletter subscription email

### Information generated through use
- Initiatives you create or express interest in
- Partnership activity and confirmed partnerships
- Notification preferences and feed visibility settings
- Account activity timestamps

### Information from third parties
We use **Supabase** for authentication. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

- Create and manage your account
- Display your profile to other users on the platform
- Facilitate connections, expressions of interest, and partnerships
- Send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset, platform notifications)
- Send newsletters you have subscribed to
- Review and approve organisation verification requests
- Improve the platform and understand how it is used
- Comply with legal obligations under Nigerian law

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019** and its implementing framework.

Our legal bases are:
- **Contract**: processing necessary to provide the platform services you have signed up for
- **Consent**: newsletter subscriptions and optional profile visibility settings
- **Legitimate interests**: platform security, abuse prevention, and service improvement
- **Legal obligation**: compliance with applicable Nigerian law

For users in the European Economic Area, we additionally rely on equivalent bases under the **GDPR**.

---

## 5. Who We Share Your Data With

We share data with the following service providers, strictly for the purpose of operating the platform:

| Provider | Purpose | Location |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Supabase | Database, authentication, file storage | USA (EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply) |
| Resend | Transactional and newsletter email delivery | USA |

We do not share your data with any other third parties unless required by law or with your explicit consent.

---

## 6. Profile Visibility

Your profile is visible to other logged-in users of the platform by default. You can control whether your activity appears on the public feed via **Settings → Privacy**.

Verified organisation badges, confirmed partnerships (if you choose to make them public), and initiative listings are visible to logged-in users.

---

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Verification documents are retained for a reasonable period after review for audit purposes.

---

## 8. Your Rights

Under the NDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Delete** your account and associated data
- **Withdraw consent** for newsletter communications at any time
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Data portability** — receive a copy of your data in a structured format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **contact@impactnatives.com**.

For users in the EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

---

## 9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and row-level security on our database.

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

---

## 10. Children

Impact Natives is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes by email or via an in-app notification. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
