# Cookie Policy

**Impact Natives Ltd**
Last updated: June 2025

---

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit and improve your experience.

---

## 2. How We Use Cookies

Impact Natives uses a minimal number of cookies. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the platform to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Supabase auth token | Keeps you signed in to your account | Session / up to 1 week |
| Supabase refresh token | Refreshes your session automatically | Up to 1 week |

These are set by Supabase, our authentication and database provider, and are required for secure login.

### Analytics Cookies (if enabled)

If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future (such as Plausible Analytics), we will update this policy. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personal data — it measures aggregate, anonymous traffic only.

We currently do not run any analytics on the platform.

### Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored by Resend, our email delivery provider. This is not a cookie — it is data you have explicitly provided. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any newsletter email.

---

## 3. What We Do Not Use

We do not use:

- Advertising or retargeting cookies
- Social media tracking pixels
- Google Analytics or similar invasive analytics
- Any third-party cookies beyond those listed above

---

## 4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in to the platform.

Most browsers allow you to:
- View cookies stored on your device
- Delete cookies individually or all at once
- Block cookies from specific sites

Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.

---

## 5. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce new cookies, we will update this policy and notify users where required. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
